Our company

Field experience backed by responsive support.

CTS was built to give site operators a reliable, knowledgeable partner for environmental compliance, inspection, testing, reporting, maintenance, and repair. For 25 years, our team has supported fueling stations, municipal facilities, and other critical operations across the Northeast.

We focus on practical solutions, clear communication, and work that keeps clients prepared for regulatory requirements without losing sight of daily operations. Whether supporting one location or a growing portfolio, CTS works to reduce risk, minimize downtime, and make compliance easier to manage.

Our mission

Make compliance safer, clearer, and easier to manage.

Our mission is to provide reliable, cost-conscious environmental compliance and site services that protect people and the environment, support safe operations, and simplify regulatory responsibilities for our clients.
