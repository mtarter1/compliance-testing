Turn requirements into everyday habits

Good training connects the rules to the actual equipment, records, alarms, and decisions employees see at the site. CTS keeps the conversation practical so operators understand what to watch, when to document, and when to ask for help.

  • Operator role and responsibility overview
  • Site-specific equipment orientation
  • Alarm and warning-condition response
  • Routine inspection awareness
  • Recordkeeping expectations
  • Escalation and contact procedures

Built around the actual site

Training is easier to remember when it reflects the facility people are responsible for. CTS can focus the session around the site's tanks, dispensers, monitoring equipment, known conditions, and recurring compliance tasks.

Plan training

Tell CTS who needs training, where the site is located, what equipment is installed, and whether there is a deadline.
