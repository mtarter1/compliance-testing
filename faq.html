Frequently asked questions

What operators ask before they schedule.

Requirements vary by site, equipment, state, and operating profile. These answers are a starting point; CTS can confirm the details that apply to your location.

What are our prices?

Pricing depends on the service, site configuration, equipment, location, and testing frequency. Contact CTS for a clear, site-specific quote.

What are our payment options and terms?

Applicable payment options and terms are included with your quote. Ask our team if your organization has purchasing, invoicing, or vendor requirements.

What are the compliance requirements for my site?

Requirements vary by state, equipment, system configuration, and due date. Share the site address and available records so CTS can help identify the services that may apply.

How far in advance will you schedule for my upcoming due date?

CTS handles urgent and routine requests. Contact us as soon as you know the deadline so we can confirm scope, preparation requirements, and an appropriate service window.

Can your tech provide service and repair during my monthly inspection?

Some issues may be addressed during the visit, while others require parts, additional time, or a separate service appointment. Tell us about any known concern when scheduling so we can prepare appropriately.

When should I expect to get my completed reports?

Report timing depends on the service and any required review or state submission. CTS will confirm the expected delivery timing for the specific work being scheduled.

Can CTS respond to emergency service calls?

Contact CTS directly with the site address, alarm or issue, equipment details, and operational impact. We will confirm availability and the fastest appropriate next step.
