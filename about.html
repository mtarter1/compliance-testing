About CTS

A compliance partner that treats every site like it matters.

For 25 years, CTS has helped independent owners, growing teams, and critical facilities keep fuel storage compliance practical, organized, and responsive.

Why customers stay for decades

CTS combines guidance, service, flexibility, and operational urgency. We work to make testing, reporting, inspections, and repairs as straightforward and cost-efficient as possible.

Our customers are not handed from one disconnected vendor to another. We aim to understand the site, communicate clearly, and remain accountable through the next step.

Regional knowledge, long-term relationships

  • Certified across MA, RI, CT, NH, VT, and ME
  • Serving sites across the Northeast
  • 300+ fueling stations
  • 60+ municipal facilities
  • 150+ towns and cities
  • 25 years in business

Who we serve

CTS supports independent gas station owners, multi-site regional operators, public works, police and fire stations, hospitals, airports, commercial fleets, schools, marinas, power generation, and other critical fueling environments.
