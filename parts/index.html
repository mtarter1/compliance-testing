What we keep on hand

Parts for service and repair.

CTS stocks commonly needed fueling-system components for installation during service calls, routine maintenance, and planned repairs.

Nozzles & dispensing hardware

Gasoline and diesel nozzles, swivels, breakaways, whip hoses, hardwall hoses, and hose-retractor components.

Filters & fuel treatment

Particulate and water-detection filters, fuel-water finding paste, microbiocide, and everyday fuel-quality supplies.

Fill & vapor hardware

Caps, adapters, gaskets, riser extensions, vent caps, monitoring caps, and overfill-related components.

Spill containment & sumps

Spill-bucket lids, seals, drain plugs, sump sensors, interstitial components, and replacement hardware.

Leak detection & monitoring

Mechanical leak detectors, tank-gauge batteries, probes, sensors, cables, lights, and monitoring accessories.

Pump, piping & repair parts

Functional elements, check valves, connectors, fittings, shear-valve parts, and system-specific replacements.
