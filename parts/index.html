Equipment categories

Start with the part family.

Competitor catalogs list hundreds of items. CTS keeps the page practical by grouping the parts customers most often need during service and repair.

Hanging hardware

Nozzles, hoses, swivels, breakaways, whip hoses, clamps, hose retractor components, and dispenser-side hardware.

Filters & fuel quality

Particulate filters, water-detection filters, filter heads, fuel-water paste, microbiocide, and everyday fuel-quality supplies.

Caps, adaptors & fill hardware

Fill caps, vapor caps, adaptors, gaskets, risers, monitoring caps, vent caps, and drop-tube related components.

Spill containment & sumps

Spill-bucket lids, seals, drain plugs, replacement hardware, sump sensors, interstitial components, and containment accessories.

Tank monitoring & leak detection

Probes, sensors, batteries, alarm lights, cables, mechanical leak detectors, and monitoring accessories.

Underground hardware

Ball valves, check valves, flex connectors, extractors, overfill protection, piping parts, shear valves, manholes, vents, and submersible components.

Pump, dispenser & repair parts

Functional elements, control boxes, relays, gaskets, fittings, pump components, and system-specific repair parts.

Special-order support

Part-number lookup, brand matching, repair planning, and sourcing support when the right component is not already on the truck.
